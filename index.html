HelioFleet and HelioField

Two open web tools for heliophysics: where the fleet is and when it lines up, and where each spacecraft connects magnetically to the Sun. Here they are in three one-minute stories. If one of them touches your work, I would like to talk.

Book 15 minutes at AGU See the three stories

When are Parker Solar Probe and Solar Orbiter next aligned?

One search finds every radial alignment, quadrature, solar conjunction and magnetic connection between any two spacecraft, past or future, and puts the map at that moment. The next Parker–Solar Orbiter alignment is on 1 December 2026, during Parker's perihelion pass.

For campaign planning, conjunction studies and following one solar wind parcel past two spacecraft.

Open this view in HelioFleet

Where does Parker Solar Probe connect to the Sun today?

A new potential-field model of the corona every day, from GONG and HMI magnetograms, with each spacecraft traced down a Parker spiral and a coronal field line to its footpoint on the Sun: the source region, its expansion factor and a WSA-type wind speed.

For source-region studies, solar energetic particle events, and choosing what to observe from Earth.

Open this view in HelioField

Which DKIST, IRIS, Hinode and Solar Orbiter observations overlap?

Every observation footprint of DKIST, IRIS, Hinode/EIS, Solar Orbiter's high-resolution instruments and sounding rockets, drawn on SDO images, with a finder for times when several facilities watched the same target. On 9 October 2024, 35 of them covered one active region.

For finding joint datasets, and planning coordinated observing with DKIST and Solar Orbiter.

What I am looking for

  • Mission and campaign teams (Parker Solar Probe, Solar Orbiter, PUNCH, IMAP, Aditya-L1, DKIST) who plan coordinated observations and could use these views, or tell me what is missing.
  • Data and software partners to bring more instruments, footprints and data services in, through HAPI, SunPy and PyHC.
  • Co-authors for a methods paper, and for studies that use the connectivity and alignment records, such as source regions of solar energetic particle events.
  • Feedback from first-time users: what you expected to find, and did not.

Also open

  • HelioFleet: the full map, sky view, timeline and research tools
  • HelioField: the 3D corona, solar-cycle series, space weather and observations
  • heliofleet-mcp: the same answers as JSON, from Python, a command line or an MCP server
  • Awesome Heliophysics: a curated list of heliophysics data, software and models
